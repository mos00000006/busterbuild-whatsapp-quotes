BUSTER BUILD INTERACTIVE FLYER - NETLIFY DEPLOYMENT

1. Unzip this archive.
2. In Netlify, open your Buster Build project > Deploys, and choose manual deploy/drag and drop the folder (or use Netlify Drop).
3. The website starts from index.html and uses flyer-1.png to flyer-4.png. Keep these five files together.
4. Test the site on a mobile phone. Tap a + over the flyer, set quantity, Add to quote, and Review WhatsApp quote.
5. The WhatsApp message opens with a prefilled request; the customer selects the contact in WhatsApp and presses Send. This file does not have a configured salespersons list or business WhatsApp number.
6. This version includes no Netlify badge in the page code. A Netlify platform badge, if supplied outside the site HTML, must be disabled in Netlify settings separately.
7. The printed PDF is unchanged. For a QR on printed flyers, point your QR to the final public website address.
8. Product buttons are positioned by page coordinates and may require small adjustments. Check each page on mobile before releasing to customers.

Promotion dates and prices are transcribed from the supplied 4-page BUSTER SALE.pdf. Please verify product variants and prices before publishing.
